The Life He Left Behind

Before Mireille, there was Sacha. Mireille was his middle name, which he later adopted as his legal first name.

They met as teenagers and grew up together, spending their twenties together before eventually eloping. When Mireille moved to New York to pursue fashion and experience ballroom on a much larger scale, his husband stayed behind. They are still married, though they no longer share a home.

In New York, Mireille founded House d’Épine and became involved in the city’s fashion and nightlife. Through the House, he established an auction business dealing in archival fashion, private sales, and pieces of questionable provenance. The schemes got bolder, including the theft of a fantasy bra valued at $50 million. His husband knows nothing about these dealings.

Mireille has built a life that bears little resemblance to the one they once imagined together. He has no intention of leaving New York.
