House d'Épine is a ballroom house with its own name to uphold. Its members compete together, represent one another at balls, and take pride in the recognition they earn. There is a strong sense of loyalty within the House, though that does little to soften the competitive streak.

On the Floor

Archive Entry Ballroom Floor

They take their categories seriously. Trophies are coveted, rivalries are personal, and losing can make for a rather unpleasant evening. Their ambitions have occasionally extended beyond the ballroom floor, with certain garments and accessories acquired through questionable means. Mireille has never been particularly troubled by the distinction between borrowing and stealing when a good look is at stake.

The House has its own routines outside competitions. Rehearsals, fittings, preparations, and gatherings keep its members occupied. They have their disagreements, and Mireille can be demanding about how things are done, but there is a genuine sisterhood between the people who make up the House.
